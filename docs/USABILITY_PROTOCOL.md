## Synthetic tasks

1. Explain what CLEAR means and whether it authorizes payment.
2. Find the one-character account change, current reference, and next safe action.
3. Identify the independently trusted callback contact, not email replacements.
4. Save/assign a request; have a separate verifier return an unverifiable case,
   then preview and record an attestation after a simulated callback.
5. Explain why HOLD plus VERIFIED can coexist. As auditor, inspect history and
   export a minimized bundle. Re-hide sensitive values before screen sharing.
6. Recover from a model timeout/missing key or stale revision; do not invent success.

Counterbalance scenario order where useful. Do not teach the answer before the
participant explains it. Stop on unsafe payment-approval assumptions. Simulated
callbacks are not evidence of real external verification.
